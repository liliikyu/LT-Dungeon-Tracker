How to play

Roll two six-sided dice and move by their sum. Leftover steps carry into the next board. Boards run left to right, then down each row.

Special icons have no effects in this version. Reaching or passing the final square completes the journey.

The routes are approximate traces of the image, not verified copies of every numbered square.
