How to move

Use +1 to +12 to move forward and −1 to −12 to move back. Movement continues across boards in either direction and stops at the beginning or end of the journey.

Enter a slot number and select Go, or press Enter, to jump to that slot, counting from the beginning of the current board. Numbers beyond this board continue across following boards. If the number exceeds the remaining journey, you stop at the final slot.

Special icons have no effects. Tile positions are approximate traces of the original image.

PATH EDITOR

Place each slot on its tile

Choose a board and slot, then click or tap the center of its tile. Use Add slot to append a missing tile, then click its center. Use Remove last slot to trim extra tiles from the end; Undo restores a removed slot. Edits apply to your game and save in this browser.

Scroll the image when zoomed in. With the map focused, arrow keys fine-tune the selected slot; Shift + arrow moves farther.

Browser edits are private to this browser. To share corrections with everyone, use Export for website and replace assets/adventure-game/routes.js in GitHub. Export backup lets you restore edits on another device.
