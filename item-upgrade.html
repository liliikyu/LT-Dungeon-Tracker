QUICK GUIDE

Plan your upgrades

Choose your goals, enter your materials, then set your priorities.

1

Choose your items

Open Battle, Specials or Gems. Select the item series and type you want to upgrade.

2

Set your stages

Current stage is where your item is now. Target stage is your goal. Mark completed items MAXED; Hide maxed keeps them out of view.

3

Enter what you own

In Summary & Inventory, enter each material quantity once. The other tabs show the amount assigned to each item.

4

Set your priorities

Lower numbers get shared materials first. Priority 1 receives what it needs before priority 2 gets any leftovers.

5

Read your progress

Solid pink shows your current stage. Soft pink shows the stage your assigned materials could reach. Materials allocated means stock reserved in your plan, not materials spent.

6

Check what is left

Needed is the total required; short is what you still need to collect. The item tabs show projected stages and estimated dungeon runs. Detailed mode shows the upgrade steps.

Estimates use materials and upgrade success rates. Check the Ely cost and your planned setup before upgrading. Update your inventory after spending materials.
